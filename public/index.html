AgriMalex Knowledge Center & Operational Manual

Guidelines on operating the platform, triggering EDRR rapid response, and executing farm prescriptions.

1. Operating the Constant AI Diagnostic Engine

AgriMalex features a built-in constant AI model. Users do not need to configure API keys. Simply:

  1. Navigate to Crop Health & Disease in the sidebar.
  2. Choose between Invasive Alien Weed or Crop Disease (Plant Doctor).
  3. Drag & drop a field photograph or sample. The system returns candidate identification, confidence index, chemical herbicide formulation (with PHI safety), mechanical grubbing protocols, and biological agents.

2. Privacy Policy & Farm Data Protection

Farm Data Ownership: All harvest yields, soil test results, and financial transactions belong strictly to your farm organization. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Ecological Public Good: Critical alien species sightings (e.g. Water Hyacinth, Prosopis) are anonymized and shared on regional EDRR maps to safeguard national waterways and grazing basins.

3. Terms of Service & Agricultural AI Disclaimer

AgriMalex AI diagnostics offer rapid decision support. Chemical pesticide and herbicide applications must comply with local agricultural regulations, PPE requirements, and product label instructions.
